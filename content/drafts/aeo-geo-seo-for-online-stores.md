Three terms come up constantly in search right now: SEO, AEO and GEO. They overlap, and they're often used as if they mean the same thing. For an online store, they're related but not identical, and the difference matters when you decide where to spend time.

## SEO: getting found in search results

Search engine optimization is the work of making a store findable in Google's results. It covers how the site is built, how the pages are written, which pages link to which, and whether other sites trust the store enough to link to it.

SEO is still the foundation. Nothing in AEO or GEO works if Google can't read the pages.

## AEO: answering questions directly

Answer engine optimization is about making a store's content easy to use as a direct answer. Google sometimes shows a short answer at the top of results, and AI tools answer questions in their own words. AEO means writing pages that answer specific questions clearly, so those answers can be pulled from your site.

For a store, that usually means clear product facts, short answers to common questions and structured information such as sizing, materials and compatibility.

## GEO: being cited by AI answer tools

Generative engine optimization is about how AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI features choose which sources to mention. These tools tend to cite sources that are clear, specific and trusted across the web.

GEO is newer and less predictable than SEO. No one can promise a placement in an AI answer, and anyone who does is overselling. What you can do is make your store easy to understand, and make sure other reliable sites mention it.

## What changes for an online store

Most of the work overlaps. The same fixes that help a page rank also help it be quoted:

- **Clear product facts.** Specifications, sizing and compatibility written plainly, in one place, on the product page.
- **Real answers to real questions.** Buying guides and FAQs that answer the question directly, in the first few lines.
- **Trust from other sites.** Mentions and links from publications and directories your buyers already use.
- **Consistency.** The same product facts on the product page, in the feed, and on any other page that mentions them.

The main difference is emphasis. SEO asks, "Can this page be found?" AEO and GEO ask, "Can this page be used as an answer?"

## What to do first

Start with what you already have. Choose your ten most important product or category pages, and check three things:

1. Does the page state the key facts clearly, near the top?
2. Does it answer the questions a buyer would ask before purchasing?
3. Is the same information consistent across the store?

Then ask an AI tool the questions your buyers ask about your category, and see which stores it mentions. That gives you a baseline. Repeat it every month, and note what changes.

## PAGE: /web-development/design

**Heading:** How we design a store that looks like your brand

Good design makes a store easy to trust and easy to buy from. It also reflects the business behind it. Many stores look like a template: the same layout, the same stock imagery and the same generic copy as thousands of others. Buyers notice, and it makes the store harder to remember.

We start by understanding the brand. That means your products, your customers, what you want them to feel, and what you want them to do. We agree those goals with you before any layout is drawn.

**What the design process includes**

- A brief that sets the goals, the audience and the brand rules.
- Layout sketches for the key pages: home, collection, product and checkout.
- A visual system covering colour, type, spacing and buttons, so every page stays consistent.
- A review with you before anything is built.
- Design files and simple rules, so your team can add pages later without breaking the look.

**Platforms**

We design for Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, Magento and WordPress. Each platform has its own limits, and the design has to work within them. We'll tell you early if a design idea won't work on your platform, and suggest a version that will.

**Design for phones first**

Most shopping now happens on phones. We design the mobile layout first, then adapt it for larger screens. That keeps the buying action easy to reach, the text easy to read and the images fast to load.

### Frequently asked questions

**Will you use our existing brand?** Yes. If you have a brand guide, we'll follow it. If you don't, we'll help you set the basics before we design.

**Can you redesign one page, or only the whole store?** Both. Many projects start with the product page, because it has the biggest effect on sales.

**Do you handle copy and photography?** We'll advise on both, and we can work with your existing photos. Professional photography is something we can recommend, but it's a separate cost.

## PAGE: /web-development/development

**Heading:** How we build and change your store

A well-built store is stable, fast and easy to change. A poorly built one breaks when an app updates, takes days to add a product and needs a developer for every small change. We build with the second problem in mind.

**What we build**

- New stores on Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, Magento and WordPress.
- Custom sections and features that your theme or platform doesn't include.
- Connections to payments, shipping, your accounting tools and your customer systems.
- Migrations from one platform to another, planned so that URLs and rankings are kept.

**How we keep the build safe**

We test each part on phones, tablets and desktops before launch. We keep changes in a test copy of the store, so the live site isn't changed until the work has been checked. Every project ends with a handover: what was built, how it works and how to make simple changes yourself.

**Why this matters for search**

A store that breaks, loads slowly or changes its URLs without planning loses rankings. The way a store is built affects how search engines read it, so development and SEO are planned together.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can you work on a store we already have?** Yes. We'll review the current build first, then agree what to change.

**Do you build apps?** Where a store needs a feature that existing apps don't cover, we can build a custom solution. We'll recommend an existing app when one does the job well.

**What happens after launch?** You can keep us on for upkeep, or hand over with documentation. The choice is yours, and it's part of the plan we agree before the work starts.

## PAGE: /web-development/conversion

**Heading:** How we turn more visitors into buyers

Most stores spend on traffic and then lose many of the visitors they paid for. The problem is usually the page, not the traffic. Product pages that don't answer questions, carts that hide the shipping cost and checkouts with too many steps all push buyers away.

Conversion work starts with measurement. We look at where shoppers leave, on phones and desktops, before we change anything.

**How we work**

1. **Measure.** We map the path from product to purchase, and find the steps where people leave.
2. **Hypothesise.** We form a clear idea of why a step loses people, based on what we see and on what shoppers tell you.
3. **Change one thing at a time.** Each change is small enough to measure. We avoid redesigning everything at once, because then you can't tell what worked.
4. **Keep what works.** Winning changes stay. Changes that don't help are reversed, and we record the reason.

**Where the gains usually come from**

- Clearer product information near the buying button.
- Shipping and returns information shown earlier.
- A simpler checkout, with fewer fields and clearer error messages.
- Product options that are easy to choose on a phone.

**What we won't do**

We won't promise a fixed percentage increase. Conversion changes depend on your products, your traffic and your buyers. We'll show you the measured results, including the changes that didn't help.

### Frequently asked questions

**Do you need access to our analytics?** Yes. Conversion work depends on measuring what happens in the store.

**How long does it take?** Measuring a store and agreeing the first changes usually takes a few weeks. Results depend on how much traffic the store gets, and we'll agree a plan that fits it.

## PAGE: /web-development/speed

**Heading:** How we make your store fast, especially on phones

Speed affects whether shoppers stay long enough to buy, and it affects search. Most slow stores have a few heavy problems, not a hundred small ones. We find those problems and fix them in order of impact.

**What we measure**

We test real pages on phones and desktops, using the same tools search engines use. We look at how quickly the main content appears, how soon the page responds to a tap and whether the layout stays still while it loads.

**What we usually fix**

- Product images that are too large or not sized for the screen they appear on.
- Scripts and apps that run on every page, even where they're not needed.
- Fonts and animations that delay the main content.
- Theme code that loads more than the page uses.

**How we keep it fast**

Speed slips over time as new apps and tracking tools are added. We agree a simple rule: nothing goes live that makes the main product pages slower. We also re-check speed on a regular schedule, which is part of our upkeep service.

### Frequently asked questions

**Will speed fixes change our design?** Usually very little. Most of the work happens behind the scenes, in images, scripts and code.

**Can you make a slow store fast in a week?** Some of the biggest gains, such as image fixes, can be quick. Other problems take longer, and we'll tell you which is which before we start.

## PAGE: /web-development/upkeep

**Heading:** Ongoing care for your store

A store needs care after launch. Platforms release updates, apps need checking, security issues come up and small changes keep arriving. When nobody owns the site, these things build up until the store breaks at the worst time.

Our upkeep service gives you one team to ask, one queue for changes and one monthly report.

**What upkeep covers**

- Platform, theme and app updates, checked on a test copy before they go live.
- Security checks, and prompt action when a problem is found.
- Regular backups, so you can recover quickly if something goes wrong.
- Monitoring, so you hear about problems before your customers do.
- Small changes and fixes, handled through a shared list.

**How requests work**

Send requests through one channel. We confirm each one, estimate the effort and agree the order. Urgent problems, such as a broken checkout, are handled first.

**The monthly report**

Every month you get a short report: what we checked, what changed, any issues and what's planned next. The report is written in plain language, so you can share it with your team.

### Frequently asked questions

**Do you charge for every small change?** Upkeep includes a set amount of small work each month. Larger changes are planned and priced separately, and we'll tell you before we start.

**Can we stop upkeep later?** Yes. When the service ends, we hand over the documentation, the access and a summary of the store's current state.
